Recreational · New Customer · No Online Order
New Recreational Customer
No Online Order
Objective
Successfully check in a first-time customer shopping for recreational use.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired government-issued ID
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Welcome in! I'll need to see your ID to get you checked in."
2Inspect ID. Check that it is not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Scan ID into Dutchie. Cover the UPC barcode with your finger. Ensure Caps Lock is off.
💡Scanner failure? Check Caps Lock first.
4Verify On-Screen Info. Confirm name, DOB, and expiration date match the physical ID.
5Check In. Click the "check in" button in the pop-up window.
6Verify Dutchie Profile. Ensure information imported correctly.
7Obtain Phone Number. Ask, "Could I get a phone number for your profile? It helps us link any future online orders to your account, and it's the only way we can contact you if you were to accidentally leave something like your ID or wallet behind."
📌This number is strictly used for order accuracy and urgent customer service regarding lost items.
8Save & Finalize Check-In. Click "save", then click "check in" on the profile page.
⚠️Don't forget the second "check in" on the profile page.
9Drag to Adult Use. Click and drag the customer from the "Intake" column to the "Adult Use" column.
10Direct to Floor. Direct the customer to the sales floor.
📌Offer assistance if they are elderly, confused, or it's their first time in any dispensary.
