New Medical Patient
No Online Order
Objective
Successfully onboard and check in a first-time patient shopping with a medical card who has not placed an online order.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID or Passport
  • Valid, unexpired Ohio Medical Marijuana Card
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Welcome! I'll need to see your ID and medical card to get you started."
2Inspect ID & Card. Check that neither has expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Scan ID into Dutchie. Cover the UPC barcode with your finger. Ensure Caps Lock is off.
💡Scanner failure? Check Caps Lock first.
4Verify On-Screen Info. Confirm name, DOB, and expiration date match the physical ID.
5Initial Check-In. Click the "check in" button in the pop-up window.
6Verify & Set Customer Type. Ensure info imported correctly. Change the "Customer Type" to "Medical" in the drop-down menu.
7Access OHMM Registry. Log in using your employee credentials.
8Bypass Scanner Prompt. When prompted for "Scanner Serial Number," enter any number and press Enter.
9Scan Medical Card. Scan the patient's Ohio Medical Marijuana Card.
⚠️We only accept Ohio cards. No reciprocity.
10Verify Card Dates. Ensure neither the Registration (Reg) nor Recommendation (Rec) date has expired.↗ Expired Medical Card Protocol
11Enter ID in OHMM. Enter the patient's ID number into the indicated field and hit Enter.
12Verify Demographics. Ensure the patient's address in the OHMM registry matches their physical ID.↗ Address Mismatch Protocol
13Scan ID to Computer. Scan the patient's ID using the naming convention: DL LASTNAME.
💡File is saved to Desktop → SCAN SNAP → EOD Folder
14Provide Intake Form. Give the patient a "New Patient Intake Form", a clipboard, and a pen. Instruct them to complete both sides.
15Update Dutchie Profile (Part 1). While patient completes the form: Copy/paste the patient's MMJ number and Rec expiration date from OHMM to their Dutchie profile.
16Upload MMJ Card Image. In OHMM, click "Download" under the digital card and select "Image". Drag-and-drop this file into the "MMJ ID" space in Dutchie.
17Upload ID Photo. In Dutchie, click the "+" in the Identification section. Click "Upload Photo" and navigate to Desktop → SCAN SNAP → EOD Folder to select the ID you just scanned. Click "Save".
18Transfer Contact Info. Copy the patient's phone number and email address from their OHMM profile to their Dutchie profile.
19Check for Discounts. In the OHMM profile, check the top-right of the Demographic section for a discount signifier (Indigent/Veteran). If present, apply the corresponding discount in the patient's Dutchie profile.
20Enter Medical Details. In Dutchie, click the "Medical" tab. Select the physician's name and enter the patient's qualifying conditions at the bottom of the page.
21Finalize Check-In. Once the patient returns the completed form, click and drag the patient from the "Intake" column to the "Medical" column.
22Direct to Sales Floor. Direct the customer to the sales floor.
📌Offer extra assistance if they are elderly, confused, or it's their first time.
23File Intake Form. Scan the signed intake form using the naming convention "INTAKE LASTNAME" and file the physical copy in the appropriate folder.
