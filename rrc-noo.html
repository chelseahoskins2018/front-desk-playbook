Returning Recreational Customer
No Online Order
Objective
Quickly and accurately check in a returning customer for recreational shopping.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID or Passport
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Welcome back! I'll need to see your ID."
2Inspect ID. Check that it is not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Scan ID into Dutchie. Cover the UPC barcode with your finger. Ensure Caps Lock is off.
💡Scanner failure? Check Caps Lock first.
4Verify On-Screen Info. Confirm name, DOB, and expiration date match the physical ID.
5Initial Check-In. Click the "check in" button in the pop-up window.
6Verify & Update Profile. Ensure the customer's information in their Dutchie profile matches their current ID. If any information (like an address) needs to be updated, do it now and click "Save".
💡This quick check ensures long-term data accuracy.
7Finalize Check-In. Ensure the "Check In" box is checked on the profile page.
8Drag to Adult Use. Click and drag the customer from the "Intake" column to the "Adult Use" column.
9Direct to Floor. "You're all set! Head on back."
