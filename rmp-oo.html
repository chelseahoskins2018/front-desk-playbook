Medical · Returning Patient · Online Order
Returning Medical Patient
With Online Order
Objective
Efficiently verify and check in a returning medical patient who has placed an online order for pickup.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID or Passport
  • Valid, unexpired Ohio Medical Marijuana Card
  • Order must be visible in Dutchie Pickups
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Hi! Picking up an online order? I'll need your ID and medical card to get started."
2Inspect ID & Card. Check that both are not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Locate Online Order. Navigate to the "Online Ready" column in Dutchie. Find the order by name or search. Click and drag the patient to the "Intake" column.
4Access OHMM Registry. Log in using your employee credentials.
5Scan Medical Card. Bypass the scanner prompt, then scan the patient's Ohio Medical Marijuana Card.
⚠️We only accept Ohio cards. No reciprocity.
6Verify Card Dates. Ensure neither the Registration (Reg) nor Recommendation (Rec) date has expired.↗ Expired Medical Card Protocol
7Verify Demographics. Ensure the patient's address in the OHMM registry matches their physical ID.↗ Address Mismatch Protocol
8Access Dutchie Profile. Click the three dots (...) on the patient's profile box and select "Profile".
9Update MMJ Card (If New). Only if the patient has a new card since their last visit:
— Download the image from OHMM.
— Drag-and-drop it into the "MMJ ID" space in Dutchie.
💡This step is often skipped for returning patients.
10Update State ID (If New). Only if the patient has a new ID:
— Scan the new ID to the computer using DL LASTNAME. The file saves to OneDrive → Desktop → SCAN SNAP → EOD Folder.
— In Dutchie, click the "+" in the Identification section and "Upload Photo". Navigate to the folder to select the ID.
💡ID not found in the folder? Ensure OneDrive is running. Look for the blue cloud icon in the taskbar.
11Verify Medical Details. Click the "Medical" tab. Ensure the recommending physician and qualifying conditions are still accurate. Click "Save" if any changes were made.
12Return to POS. Click the "Dutchie" symbol in the top left to return to the main POS screen.
13Finalize Check-In. Click and drag the patient's profile from the "Intake" column to the "Medical" column.
14Direct to Pickup. "Your order is all set and ready for you!" Direct them to the pickup area.
