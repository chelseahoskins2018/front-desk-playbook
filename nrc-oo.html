Recreational · New Customer · Online Order
New Recreational Customer
With Online Order
Objective
Create a Dutchie profile for a first-time recreational customer and process their online pickup order.
Core Principle
The online order system does not auto-populate profiles for new customers. Manually verify all demographics.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID or Passport
  • Order is placed and visible in the "New Patient" or "Online Ready" column
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Hi! Picking up an online order? I'll need to see your ID to get you checked in."
2Inspect ID. Check that it is not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Locate Online Order. Navigate to the "Online Ready" or "New Patient" column in Dutchie. Find the order by name or search. Click and drag the customer to the "Intake" column.
4Access Dutchie Profile. Click the three dots (...) on the profile box and select "Profile".
5Explain the Process. "I just need to quickly set up your profile for you, it'll only take a moment."
💡This reassures the customer there isn't a problem.
6Manually Verify/Enter Demographics. The online order system does not auto-populate profiles for new customers. Manually ensure the following fields are 100% accurate and match the physical ID:
— Legal Name
— Date of Birth
— ID Number
— ID Expiration Date
— Full Address
⚠️Accuracy is critical for compliance. Double-check every field.
7Verify Phone Number. Ask the customer, "Can you please verify the last 4 digits of the phone number on file for your order?"
💡This is a quick way to ensure their contact info is correct.
8Save Profile. Click the "Save" button at the top of the customer's Dutchie profile.
9Return to POS. Click the "Dutchie" symbol in the top left to return to the main POS screen.
10Finalize Check-In. Click and drag the customer from the "Intake" column to the "Adult Use" column.
11Direct to Pickup. "You're all set! Your order is ready for you." Direct them to the pickup/sales floor.
