Recreational · Returning Customer · Online Order
Returning Recreational Customer
With Online Order
Objective
Quickly and accurately verify a returning recreational customer and process their online pickup order.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID or Passport
  • Order must be visible in Dutchie Pickups
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Hi there! Picking up an online order?"
2Inspect ID. Check that it is not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Locate Online Order. Navigate to the "Online Ready" column in Dutchie. Find the order by name or by searching their last name in the search bar. Click and drag the customer to the "Intake" column.
4Verify Profile & ID Match. Quickly verify that the information in the customer's Dutchie profile matches their physical ID. Key points to check:
— Name
— Address
— ID Number
— ID Expiration Date
— Date of Birth
💡This is a critical compliance step. Do not skip it.
5Update Profile (If Needed). If any information needs to be updated:
— Click the three dots (...) on the profile box and select "Profile".
— Make the necessary edits.
— Click the "Save" button at the top of the page.
— Click the "Dutchie" symbol to return to the POS.
6Finalize Check-In. Click and drag the customer's profile from the "Intake" column to the "Adult Use" column.
7Direct to Pickup. "You're all set! Your order is ready for you at the back." Direct them to the pickup/sales floor.
