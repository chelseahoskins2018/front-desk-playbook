Problematic ID Handling
Purpose
Guide for handling invalid, expired, or problematic forms of identification at the front desk. Empathy and clarity are required, but compliance is non-negotiable.
← All Procedures
ID IssueAction RequiredScript / Notes
Expired IDSTOP. Transaction cannot proceed."I'm sorry, but I cannot accept an expired ID for verification. Do you have a valid, unexpired ID or passport with you today?"
No Physical IDSTOP. Transaction cannot proceed."I apologize, but I must physically scan a valid government-issued ID to check you in. We cannot accept digital photos or photocopies."
Under 21 (Recreational)STOP. Entry is prohibited."I'm sorry, you must be 21 years or older to enter the dispensary for recreational use."
Under 21 (Medical) & Invalid/Expired CardSTOP. Entry is prohibited."I'm sorry, your medical card is [expired/invalid]. To enter the premises, you must have a valid medical card, as you are under 21."
Out-of-State or Foreign IDCHECK TYPE.✅ ACCEPT:
— Valid, unexpired U.S. State Driver's License/ID Card.
— Valid, unexpired Foreign Passport.

❌ DO NOT ACCEPT:
— Any foreign driver's license.

"I can accept your out-of-state license, but I cannot accept a foreign driver's license. A passport would work."

💡Educational literature is available at the front desk for questions.
Cracked/Damaged IDUSE JUDGEMENT. Follow One-Time Exception Protocol if functional.1. Assess: If the barcode scans and all info is legible, it may be acceptable.
2. State Guidance: Per the State of Ohio, a cracked ID is grounds for refusal. A crack over the barcode will likely cause a scan failure.
3. Our Protocol: We allow a one-time exception. If you accept it, you MUST complete the following:
— Notate Dutchie Profile: In the Medical tab, Journal, create a new entry.
— Subject: ID Guidance Given [mm/dd/yy]
— Body: State ID Guidance given [mm/dd/yy]. [Your Initials]. Customer advised to obtain replacement ID from BMV.
— Inform the Customer: "We can make a one-time exception today. For your next visit, you will need an undamaged ID. You can get a temporary paper copy from the BMV, which we can accept for up to 28 days."
Name MismatchREQUEST SUPPORTING DOCS. Advise on Physician Update.The name on the ID must match the name on the medical card. A marriage certificate or other legal document can be used to resolve the discrepancy.

Crucially, inform the patient: "You will also need to inform your recommending physician of your name change to ensure your medical records are accurate."
📌When in doubt, escalate to a manager.
BMV Temporary Paper IDACCEPT.We accept temporary paper IDs issued by the Ohio BMV, provided they are within 28 days of the issue date.
