Address Mismatch Protocol
Purpose
Resolve discrepancies between a patient's address in the OHMM registry and their physical ID, ensuring full state compliance.
Core Rule
The address in the OHMM registry must match the physical ID. There are no exceptions for medical dispensing.
Key Fact
Address and ID updates made by a patient in their OHMM registry take effect immediately.

Scenario 1 & 4: Standard Resolution Path
(For an Ohio ID with an OHMM address mismatch, or an OHMM profile with an old out-of-state ID.)
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Identify Mismatch. "I'm showing your [address/ID information] in our state registry as [OHMM Info], but your ID shows [ID Info]. To proceed medically, these need to match."
2Guide Patient to Update. "The quickest way to fix this is to log into your Ohio Medical Marijuana Registry account right now and update your information. The change is instant, and then we can proceed."
3Provide Login Assistance. If they struggle, provide their registered email address (write it down discreetly after verifying their identity). Assist with password reset if needed, showing compassion.
4Resolution & Alternative. If they update it: "Perfect, it's updated. You're all set to shop medically."
If they cannot/will not: "Since we can't dispense medically with the mismatch, you're welcome to shop under our recreational guidelines today if you'd like."
Scenario 2: Out-of-State ID, Ohio OHMM Address
(The patient has an out-of-state ID but an Ohio address in their OHMM profile.)
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Identify Situation. "I see you have an Ohio address in the system, but an out-of-state ID. To verify your Ohio residency, I need to see two additional documents with your name and this Ohio address."
2Request Proof of Residency. Direct the patient to the official list of approved documents.
3Verify & Upload. Inspect the documents. As a courtesy, scan and upload them to the patient's Dutchie profile in the Identification section.
4Notate the Profile. Add a journal note: [Date]: Verified OH residency with [List Documents]. Out-of-state ID on file. [Your Initials]
5Encourage ID Update. "To make future visits smoother, we encourage you to update your driver's license with the Ohio BMV if you're a permanent resident."
Scenario 3: Ohio ID, Out-of-State OHMM Address
(The patient has an Ohio ID, but their OHMM profile lists an out-of-state address.)
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Identify Compliance Issue.
🛑This is non-compliant.
"I see your OHMM profile has an out-of-state address, but your ID is from Ohio. The state requires a valid Ohio address in your registry profile to dispense medically."
2Guide Patient to Update. "You can log into your OHMM registry right now to update your address to your Ohio one. It will update instantly, and then we can proceed medically."
3Provide Login Assistance. Offer the same assistance as in Scenario 1.
4Resolution & Alternative. If they update it: Proceed with medical check-in.
If they cannot/will not: "You are welcome to shop recreationally today if you'd like."

Universal Tools & Exceptions

  • BMV Change of Address Card: A Change of Address card from the Ohio BMV is an acceptable form of address verification. Scan and upload it to the patient's Dutchie profile.
  • Final Resort: If the patient is unable or unwilling to resolve the mismatch on the spot, they cannot be dispensed to medically. Their options are: (1) Shop recreationally if over 21. (2) Return another time after the issue has been resolved.
