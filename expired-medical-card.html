Expired Medical Card Protocol
Purpose
Guide the front desk through the sensitive process of handling a patient with an expired medical card, ensuring compliance while providing compassionate, helpful service to get them back on track.
← All Procedures

Registration (Reg) Date ExpiredSTOP. Entry is prohibited. No exceptions.OFFER RENEWAL ASSISTANCE. Patient cannot shop medically until renewed.1. Explain & Offer Help: "I see your card's registration has expired. The good news is, you can renew this yourself online right now. Would you like to use your phone to do it? I can guide you through the steps."
2. Provide Resources: Offer the paper supplemental guide. Website: www.ohiomedicalmarijuanaregistry.com
3. Assist with Dignity: If they struggle, offer to help with the Google search. If they can't remember their email, write it down for them discreetly after verifying their identity. Help with password reset within the bounds of the law.
4. Offer Alternative: "Once it's renewed, you're all set. Since you're over 21, you're also welcome to shop under our recreational guidelines today if you choose to wait to renew."
Recommendation (Rec) Date ExpiredSTOP. Entry is prohibited. No exceptions.OFFER RECREATIONAL PATH. Patient cannot shop medically.1. Explain: "I see your doctor's recommendation has expired. To shop medically, you'll need to get a new recommendation from your doctor."
2. Offer Alternative: "Since you're over 21, you're welcome to shop under our recreational guidelines today."
3. Encourage Shopping Around: "A lot of doctors change their fees. When you're looking for a new recommendation, we encourage you to call around. You might find a better price than what you paid last time. Groupon is a good resource."
4. Provide Guidance: "You'll need the new recommendation from any certified doctor before you can renew your card online."
Both Reg & Rec ExpiredSTOP. Entry is prohibited. No exceptions.OFFER RECREATIONAL PATH. Patient cannot shop medically.1. Explain: "It looks like both your card registration and your doctor's recommendation have expired."
2. Offer Alternative: "Since you're over 21, you can shop recreationally today."
3. Provide Guidance: "You'll need to get a new recommendation from your doctor first, and then you can renew your card online at www.ohiomedicalmarijuanaregistry.com."

Universal Principles & Notations

  • Compassion is Key: "Give them grace and remember that you will be that age someday too." Assist elderly patients and those in need, as long as it doesn't disrupt patient flow.
  • HIPAA Compliance: When providing a remembered email address, write it down discreetly only after you have fully verified the patient's identity.
  • If the patient chooses the recreational path, follow the appropriate New or Returning Recreational Customer process.
  • If the patient is under 21, the interaction must end. The script is: "I'm sorry, but without a valid medical card, I cannot allow anyone under 21 to enter the dispensary. You'll need to renew your card before your next visit."
  • Notate the Profile: Add a journal note: [Date]: Advised of expired [Reg/Rec/Both]. Provided renewal assistance. [Your Initials]
