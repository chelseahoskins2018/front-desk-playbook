Medical · New Patient · Online Order
New Medical Patient
With Online Order
Objective
Successfully onboard a first-time medical patient who has placed an online order. Combines the full medical intake process with verifying a pre-order.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID or Passport
  • Valid, unexpired Ohio Medical Marijuana Card
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Hi! Picking up an online order? I'll need your ID and medical card to get started."
2Inspect ID & Card. Check that both are not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Locate Online Order. Navigate to the "Online Ready" or "New Patient" column in Dutchie. Find the order by name or search. Click and drag the patient to the "Intake" column.
4Access Dutchie Profile. Click the three dots (...) on the profile box and select "Profile".
5Manually Verify/Enter Demographics. The online order system does not auto-populate profiles for new patients. Manually ensure the legal name, date of birth, ID number, ID expiration date, and full address are 100% accurate and match the physical ID.
⚠️The patient MUST use the form of ID on file with the State of Ohio (State ID, Driver's License, Passport, Birth Certificate).
6Set Customer Type & Save. Change the "Customer Type" to "Medical". Click the "Save" button at the top of the page.
7Access OHMM Registry. Log in using your employee credentials.
8Scan Medical Card. Bypass the scanner prompt, then scan the patient's Ohio Medical Marijuana Card.
⚠️We only accept Ohio cards. No reciprocity.
9Verify Card Dates. Ensure neither the Registration (Reg) nor Recommendation (Rec) date has expired.↗ Expired Medical Card Protocol
10Verify Demographics. Ensure the patient's address in the OHMM registry matches their physical ID.↗ Address Mismatch Protocol
11Scan ID to Computer. Scan the patient's ID using the naming convention: DL LASTNAME. (Saves to OneDrive → Desktop → SCAN SNAP → EOD Folder).
💡ID not found? Ensure the OneDrive blue cloud icon is in the taskbar.
12Provide Intake Form. Give the patient a "New Patient Intake Form", a clipboard, and a pen. Instruct them to complete both sides.
13Update Dutchie Profile (Part 1). While patient completes the form: Copy/paste the patient's MMJ number and Rec expiration date from OHMM to their Dutchie profile.
14Upload MMJ Card Image. In OHMM, click "Download" under the digital card and select "Image". Drag-and-drop this file into the "MMJ ID" space in Dutchie.
15Upload ID Photo. In Dutchie, click the "+" in the Identification section. Click "Upload Photo" and navigate to OneDrive → Desktop → SCAN SNAP → EOD Folder to select the ID you just scanned. Click "Save".
16Transfer Contact Info. Copy the patient's phone number and email address from their OHMM profile to their Dutchie profile.
17Check for Discounts. In the OHMM profile, check the top-right of the Demographic section for a discount signifier (Indigent/Veteran). If present, apply the corresponding discount in the patient's Dutchie profile.
18Enter Medical Details. In Dutchie, click the "Medical" tab. Select the physician's name and enter the patient's qualifying conditions at the bottom of the page.
19Finalize Check-In. Once the patient returns the completed form, click and drag the patient from the "Intake" column to the "Medical" column.
20Direct to Sales Floor. "Your order is all set! Head to the back and they'll have your products ready for you."
📌Offer extra assistance if they are elderly, confused, or it's their first time.
21File Intake Form. Scan the signed intake form using the naming convention "INTAKE LASTNAME" and file the physical copy in the appropriate folder.
