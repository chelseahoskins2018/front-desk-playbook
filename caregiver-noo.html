Medical Caregiver
No Online Order
Objective
Verify a registered caregiver and check in their patient for a shopping visit where no online order exists.
Core Principle
The caregiver's credentials access the patient's profile. The patient's file is checked for validity, and the caregiver is formally assigned in the POS.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID (caregiver)
  • Valid, unexpired Ohio Medical Marijuana Caregiver Card
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Welcome! Who are you shopping for today?"
2Inspect Caregiver's ID & Card. Check that both are not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Access Patient via OHMM. Log into OHMM. Scan the caregiver's medical card and enter the caregiver's state ID number.
4Select Patient. From the menu that appears, select the correct patient you are shopping for.
5Verify Caregiver Link. In the patient's OHMM profile, navigate to the "Caregiver" tab. Confirm the caregiver's information matches the person in front of you.
6Perform Patient Medical Checks. Perform the standard returning medical patient checks using the patient's OHMM profile and Dutchie file. Check:
— Patient's Card Dates (Reg & Rec) in OHMM.
— Patient's Demographics in OHMM vs Dutchie.
⚠️We do not have the patient's physical cards. We are relying on the file.
7Update Patient's Dutchie Profile, if necessary.
— MMJ Card: Update if the patient's card in OHMM is new.
— State ID: The caregiver must provide the patient's current, unexpired ID. If the ID on file is expired and unavailable, medical dispensing is prohibited.
📌If the patient's ID is expired and unavailable, the caregiver can shop recreationally for themselves, but not for the patient.
8Verify Caregiver in Dutchie. In the patient's Dutchie profile (Medical tab → Caregivers section), verify the caregiver's demographics match their physical ID.
9Locate & Assign in Dutchie. Use the Dutchie search bar to find the patient's profile. On the profile box, click the "Caregiver" button and select the correct caregiver from the dropdown.
10Update Days Supply. Click the pencil icon next to "Days Supply Remaining" and update from the OHMM Purchase Summary.
11Finalize Check-In. Ensure all changes are saved. On the main POS, drag the patient's profile from "Intake" to "Medical".
12Direct to Floor. "You're all set to shop for [Patient Name]. Head on back!"
