Returning Medical Patient
No Online Order
Objective
Verify a returning medical patient's active status and check them in for a shopping visit.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID or Passport
  • Valid, unexpired Ohio Medical Marijuana Card
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet & Identify. "Welcome back! I'll need your ID and medical card today."
2Inspect ID & Card. Check that both are not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Scan ID into Dutchie. Cover the UPC barcode with your finger. Ensure Caps Lock is off.
💡Scanner failure? Check Caps Lock first.
4Verify On-Screen Info. Confirm name, DOB, and expiration date match the physical ID.
5Initial Check-In. Click the "check in" button in the pop-up window.
6Verify Dutchie Profile. Ensure the information in their profile matches their current ID. Update the address or other details if needed. Click "Save".
7Access OHMM Registry. Log in using your employee credentials.
8Scan Medical Card. Bypass the scanner prompt, then scan the patient's Ohio Medical Marijuana Card.
⚠️We only accept Ohio cards. No reciprocity.
9Verify Card Dates. Ensure neither the Registration (Reg) nor Recommendation (Rec) date has expired.↗ Expired Medical Card Protocol
10Verify Demographics. Ensure the patient's address in the OHMM registry matches their physical ID.↗ Address Mismatch Protocol
11Update MMJ Card (If New). Only if the patient has a new card since their last visit:
— Download the image from OHMM.
— Drag-and-drop it into the "MMJ ID" space in Dutchie.
💡This step is often skipped for returning patients.
12Update State ID (If New). Only if the patient has a new ID:
— Scan the new ID to the computer using DL LASTNAME.
— In Dutchie, click the "+" in the Identification section and "Upload Photo". Navigate to the folder to select the ID.
💡ID not found? Ensure the OneDrive blue cloud icon is in the taskbar.
13Verify Medical Details. Click the "Medical" tab. Ensure the recommending physician and qualifying conditions are still accurate. Click "Save" if any changes were made.
14Finalize Check-In. On the main profile page, ensure the "Check In" box is checked. Click and drag the patient's profile from the "Intake" column to the "Medical" column.
15Direct to Floor. "You're all set! Head on back."
