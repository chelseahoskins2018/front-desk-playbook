Medical Caregiver
With Online Order
Objective
Use a caregiver's credentials to access, verify, and pick up a patient's online order.
Core Principle
The caregiver's ID and card pull up the patient's profile in OHMM. Medical checks are performed on the patient's file, not physical cards.
Prerequisites
  • Valid, unexpired State ID (caregiver)
  • Valid, unexpired Ohio Medical Marijuana Caregiver Card
  • Patient's online order is visible in Dutchie
← All Procedures
StepActionNotes / Links / Tips
1Greet, Identify & Locate Order. "Hello! Picking up an online order for [Patient Name]? I'll need your ID and caregiver card." Locate the patient's order in the "Online Ready" column and drag it to "Intake".
2Inspect Caregiver's ID & Card. Check that both are not expired.↗ Problematic ID Handling
3Access Patient via OHMM. Log into OHMM. Scan the caregiver's medical card and enter the caregiver's state ID number.
4Select Patient. From the menu that appears, select the correct patient you are picking up for.
5Verify Caregiver Link. In the patient's OHMM profile, navigate to the "Caregiver" tab. Confirm the caregiver's information matches the person in front of you.
6Perform Patient Medical Checks. Now perform the standard returning medical patient checks using the patient's OHMM profile and Dutchie file. Check:
— Patient's Card Dates (Reg & Rec) in OHMM.
— Patient's Demographics in OHMM vs Dutchie.
⚠️We do not have the patient's physical cards. We are relying on the file.
7Update Patient's Dutchie Profile, if necessary.
— MMJ Card: Update if the patient's card in OHMM is new.
— State ID: The caregiver must provide the patient's current, unexpired ID. If the patient's ID on file is expired and unavailable, medical dispensing is prohibited.
📌If the patient's ID is expired and unavailable, the caregiver can shop recreationally for themselves, but not for the patient.
8Verify Caregiver in Dutchie. In the patient's Dutchie profile (Medical tab → Caregivers section), verify the caregiver's demographics match their physical ID.
9Update Days Supply & Notate. Update the patient's days supply in Dutchie from the OHMM data. Add a medical note: Caregiver [Name] picked up order on [Date]. [Your Initials]
10Finalize Check-In. Ensure all changes are saved. On the main POS, drag the patient's profile from "Intake" to "Medical".
11Direct to Pickup. "Everything is verified and your order is ready at the back."
